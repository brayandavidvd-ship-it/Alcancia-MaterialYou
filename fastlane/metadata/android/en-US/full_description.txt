Alcancía v1.3.0 is a fast, private, minimalist personal finance app for Android, built with Jetpack Compose and Material 3.

Key features:
• 100% offline finance tracking: income, expenses, and savings transactions stored locally on your device using SQLite (Room), with no required accounts.
• Savings goals with progress ring: pin your priority goal on Home, log deposits or withdrawals with an integrated keypad, and review your full goal history.
• Smart shopping lists: organize multiple lists, set per-list budgets, check off items, reuse recent prices, and log totals as expenses with one tap.
• Optional Gemini cloud assistant: enable when desired using your own API key to log transactions, goals, or shopping items via text or voice dictation.
• Accessibility & customization: colorblind preset, accent palettes, light/dark/system themes, density controls, and numeric typography options.
• Built-in security: biometric fingerprint lock and configurable PIN code.
• Customizable local reminders: schedule time, frequency (daily, weekdays, or weekends), and edit your notification message.
• Free & open-source with zero ads or trackers: no hidden analytics and full local JSON backups under your control.
